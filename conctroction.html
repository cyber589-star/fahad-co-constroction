How We Work

Our Build Process

A structured, transparent process with progress you can see — and a written report at every stage.

01

Site Visit & Estimate

We visit your land, measure it properly and submit an itemized estimate with material quantities.

02

Design & Approvals

We help with HCCB/DC drawings, design selection and secure your municipal approvals.

03

Construction

Formwork, RCC, masonry, plastering and MEP work executed on a published site schedule.

04

Handover & As-Built

Final cleaning, as-built drawings, warranties on workmanship and a walkthrough of your home.
